# Team Collaboration in FlutterFlow (2026 Guide)

Mastering **flutterflow team collaboration** is essential for building production-ready mobile and web applications with FlutterFlow. In this comprehensive 2026 guide, you will learn the exact architectural principles, step-by-step implementation workflows, and battle-tested best practices developed by KinetixSoft engineers.

A Guide to Collaborating on FlutterFlow
Projects
Welcome to the team! In FlutterFlow, sharing your work and building apps collectively is
seamless. This guide outlines how project sharing works, how to invite external users, how to
hand over project ownership, and how real-time teamwork functions.
1. What is Project Collaboration?
To effectively collaborate, you need to understand the structural types of projects, external
access options, and visibility tools available in FlutterFlow:
● Team Projects: Open workspaces accessible to everyone in your organization. All
members automatically join as Editors, though they can be manually downgraded to
Viewers.
● Restricted Team Projects: Private workspaces hidden from the rest of the organization
until specific team members are manually selected and added.
● Personal Projects: Private, isolated workspaces dedicated solely to you. Your editing
capabilities depend entirely on your personal subscription plan.
● Collaborator Pass: A paid add-on that grants an external user (like a contractor or
client) full Editor access to a specific project within your team workspace.
● Project Activity Log: A running history of edits that tracks who made what changes
over time, providing visibility into overall project progress.

2. Why use FlutterFlow Collaboration Features?
Utilizing these collaboration frameworks provides the control and security necessary for
professional app development:
● Security & IP Protection: By using Restricted Team Projects, you ensure sensitive or
unannounced client projects remain invisible to unauthorized internal staff.
● Cost Efficiency: Read-Only access is free, allowing you to loop in unlimited clients for
feedback without inflating your software bill.
● Seamless Handovers: Native ownership transfers allow you to seamlessly hand off a
completed app directly to a client's corporate FlutterFlow account.
● Accountability: The Project Activity Log removes the guesswork from team
management, showing you exactly who modified the app and when.
3. How to Manage Workspace Collaboration
Sharing & Managing Access
1. Internal Sharing: Navigate to your project's Collaboration settings and utilize the
Share with team dropdown menu to toggle between Team, Restricted, or Personal.
○ Note: Team sharing features require a Growth plan or higher.
○ Note: The designated Team Owner always maintains full administrative control
over every project, regardless of who originally created it.

-- 2 of 4 --

2. External Sharing: Invite external stakeholders via the email invite field.
○ For Viewers: Type their email and assign "Read Only".
○ For External Editors: The Team Owner must purchase a Collaborator Pass.
The recipient must already have an active paid FlutterFlow plan (Basic, Growth,
or Business) and verify their email to clear their "Pending" status.
○ Note: "Library" projects cannot be converted into Restricted team projects.
●
Transferring Project Ownership
When a project is fully completed and ready for final offboarding:
1. Navigate to Settings & Integrations > Project Setup > Collaboration > Project-Level
Access.
2. Locate the recipient's name, click their current role dropdown, and select Owner.
3. ⚠ Warning: This action is immediate and cannot be undone. You will instantly lose
control of the project. The recipient must have an active paid plan to accept ownership.
Real-Time Live Development
● Co-Editing: If your team is on a Growth plan or higher, multiple developers can build
on the exact same page simultaneously. You will see live mouse cursors and
modifications in real time without workspace interruption.
● Reviewing History: To audit project history, access the Project Activity Log.
○ Note: This history tracking is an exclusive premium feature available only to
Enterprise workspaces.
